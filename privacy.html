SparkKeys Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

SparkKeys is a piano learning app. This page explains what information we keep about you, why we keep it, and how you can remove it.

What we store

When you create an account, we store your email address so you can sign in. If you sign in with Google, Google also shares your name and profile picture with us, and we store those alongside your email.

We also store your learning progress, such as which lessons and songs you have practised and how you went. This lets you pick up where you left off on any device.

Your microphone

SparkKeys asks for microphone access so it can hear which notes you play. The sound is processed on your own device, inside your browser. It is never recorded, uploaded or sent to us or anyone else. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your browser settings and still use the rest of the app.

Where your information is kept

Your account and progress are stored using Google Firebase, a cloud service run by Google. Google's servers may be located outside Australia. Your account is set up so that only you can read or change your own progress.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the service providers needed to run the app (Google Firebase for sign-in and storage).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app. Deleting it permanently removes your email and your learning progress. If you can't access the app, email us at the address below and we will delete it for you.

Contact

If you have any questions about your privacy, or want to see or correct the information we hold about you, email sparkkeys.help@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
